What is PU?

PU stands for Polyurethane paint - a finish applied on top of the base material to give the panel a royal, luxurious feel. It is highly durable, resistant to heat, chemicals and water, making it ideal for kitchens. Crucially, it is scratch resistant, which is why this is the finish to specify for clients who want longevity in high-use spaces.

Designer notes

!

Shutter clearance. Always account for a 3 mm clearance when working with 25 mm thick shutters. Failing to do this causes binding on installation.

!

Groove profile is shutters only. Do not specify groove profile for fillers or skirting. Only plain profile should be used for those.

!

No customisation. The sizes shown are standards. Width and height outside the published ranges are not available - do not promise these to clients.
